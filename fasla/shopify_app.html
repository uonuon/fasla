1. Read your orders

Fasla reads order lines only: date, product, size, colour, quantity, price and status. It never reads customer names, emails, phones or addresses.

Shopify lets new apps read only the last 60 days of orders. For your full history, export it in Shopify (Orders → Export → All orders, CSV) and upload the file here. Fasla keeps only the columns it needs and drops customer details before saving.
